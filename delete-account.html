KirayaDiary - Account Deletion

How to Delete Your Account

Option 1: Delete from the App

  1. Open KirayaDiary app
  2. Go to the Profile tab
  3. Scroll down and tap "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm the deletion in the dialog

Option 2: Request via Email

Send an email to sharma.yash520@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your KirayaDiary account.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. The following data will be deleted:

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • Your account profile (name, email, profile photo)
  • All tasks and reminders you created
  • Tenant records and rent payment history
  • Group memberships
  • Push notification tokens

Data Retention

Once your deletion request is processed, all your personal data will be permanently removed from our servers within 30 days. Shared group data that other members depend on may be anonymized rather than deleted.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are typically processed within 7 business days.

Privacy Policy for KirayaDiary

Last updated: March 26, 2026

Introduction

KirayaDiary ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

Information We Collect

When you use KirayaDiary, we collect the following information:

  • Account Information: When you sign in with Google, we collect your name, email address, and profile photo URL.
  • User-Generated Content: Tasks, reminders, tenant details, and rent records that you create within the app.
  • Device Information: Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) tokens for push notifications.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the app functionality
  • To enable collaborative group features with family members
  • To send push notifications for task reminders and rent due dates
  • To sync your data across devices

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely in Google Firebase (Firestore) and is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS. Only you and the members of your groups can access your shared data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your data is only shared with members of the groups you create or join within the app.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: For authentication, data storage, and push notifications
  • Google Sign-In: For user authentication

Account Deletion

You can request account deletion directly from the app by going to Profile and tapping "Delete Account". Upon confirmation, your deletion request will be submitted and your account data will be permanently removed. You can also request deletion by emailing us at sharma.yash520@gmail.com.

Children's Privacy

KirayaDiary is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.

Contact Us

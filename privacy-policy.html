Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

In short: Trakora stores what you add (tasks, goals, rent, expenses and so on) so you and the people in your groups can see it. We don't sell your data or show ads. Features like fingerprint lock, reading copied text and (in one special version) reading bank SMS work on your phone; nothing is uploaded unless you choose to save it.

1. Who we are

Trakora ("we", "us") is an app for families and small groups to share tasks, goals, rent, expenses, maintenance, inventory and attendance. Contact: sharma.yash520@gmail.com.

3. Things that stay on your phone

  • App lock. Fingerprint, face or PIN unlock uses your phone's own security. Trakora never receives or stores your biometric data.
  • Reminders. Scheduled on your device from the tasks, rent and maintenance dates you've entered.
  • Home-screen widget. A short summary of today's tasks is kept on your device for the widget.
  • Settings. Preferences such as vibration, motion, language and app lock are stored only on your device.
  • Camera. Used only when you choose to scan an invite QR code or take a group photo. Scanned images are not saved or uploaded.

4. Adding expenses from payments

  • Share to Trakora. If you share a message from another app into Trakora, we read that text to pre-fill an expense. Nothing is saved unless you save the expense.
  • Copied payments. When you open "Add expense", Trakora checks your clipboard once, on your device, to offer a copied payment. The clipboard is not stored or uploaded; only an expense you save is.
  • Bank statement import. A CSV statement you pick is read on your device. The file itself is never uploaded; only the transactions you select are saved as expenses.
  • Bank SMS (optional; only in the version distributed outside Google Play). The Google Play version of Trakora does not request SMS access. In the separately distributed version, you can turn on "Auto-detect expenses from bank SMS". It is off by default. When on, the app reads SMS from banks and payment apps on your phone to find transactions, and shows them to you for review. Only transactions you confirm are saved as expenses. Message text is never uploaded, and you can turn the feature off and revoke the permission at any time.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising. Your content is visible only to members of the groups you create or join. When you share a rent receipt, report or invite, you choose where it goes (for example WhatsApp); that app's own privacy policy then applies.

6. Service providers

  • Google Firebase: sign-in (Authentication), database (Cloud Firestore, stored in the asia-south1 region, Mumbai), crash reports (Crashlytics), app integrity (App Check), and notification delivery (Cloud Messaging).
  • Google Sign-In: account sign-in.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest by Google Firebase. Database security rules limit each group's data to its members and private goals to their owner.

8. Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete individual items at any time. To delete your account, go to Profile → Delete account in the app, or email us. We then remove your account and personal data. Content you added to a group that still has other members may remain visible to them, without your account details, unless you ask us to remove it. Crash reports are kept for up to 90 days.

9. Children

Trakora is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Your choices

  • Turn notifications, app lock or SMS detection on and off in the app or in your phone's settings.
  • Leave a group, or delete items, goals or your account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct or delete it: sharma.yash520@gmail.com.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above. For significant changes we'll also tell you in the app.
